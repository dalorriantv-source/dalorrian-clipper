# Dalorrian Clipper - Privacy Policy

Dalorrian Clipper is a personal tool used only by Dalorrian (dalorriantv@gmail.com) to manage his own YouTube channels.

What it does with Google / YouTube data: it uploads videos to Dalorrian's own channels, edits the titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists of his own videos, and reads his own channel's video statistics.

It does not collect, sell, or share any data with anyone. Login tokens stay on Dalorrian's own computer. No data from any other person's account is accessed.

To stop access at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Contact: dalorriantv@gmail.com
